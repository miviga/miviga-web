Miviga is a messaging application built on the principle that privacy is not a feature, it is a right. Currently under development.

01

End-to-End Encryption

Built on X3DH and Double Ratchet. The encryption core is built and written in Rust.

02

Minimal Data

Username-based accounts. Phone numbers are kept only as a one-way hash, so they cannot be read.

03

Early Stage

Foundation is built, chat screens are in progress. Not released and not yet audited.

A new standard for private communication.

Miviga is being built from the ground up with privacy as its foundation. Group chats, calls, safety numbers and multiple devices are planned after the first release.
